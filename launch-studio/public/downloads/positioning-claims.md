# Openline: the selected orange story

## Scope

Only Original orange remains. The actual icon accompanies the Openline name,
white on orange and black on cream. The underscore wordmark is not used.
Frames 1–4 cover destination, purchase confirmation, setup and eSIM collection.
All six use distinct supplied app UI, alternating orange and warm cream.

## Screenshot 5: network flexibility

**More networks. More ways to connect.**

Multiple leading network partners. Another way to keep you connected.

The visual uses the supplied connection dashboard, including its existing
Connected badge and data card. No new signal readings or carrier logos are
invented. A Connected badge does not independently prove superior signal or
multi-network access. A profile replacement is best effort and may require
setup; available alternatives depend on the location and plan.

This reflects Paul's explanation on 4 October 2026: Openline can sometimes
provision another eSIM profile and underlying provider when a better service is
needed, and may try an alternative infrastructure if unlimited-plan fair use is
repeatedly restrictive. It is not a promise of instant, invisible or uninterrupted
switching. Unlimited plans remain subject to the local network's fair-use rules.

## Screenshot 6: OMDM and value

**Smarter market. Better prices.**

Our Openline Mobile Data Market (OMDM) compares supplier offers to help bring you lower-priced travel data.

The visual uses supplied plan-selection UI with duration and price choices.
These sample figures are not savings evidence. Both platforms now explicitly
connect better-price positioning to OMDM supplier comparison on the final image.
This replaces the rejected neutral plan-choice ending. The description paragraph
also retains the OMDM explanation. Both require launch evidence before submission.

This is owner-provided positioning for the Openline Mobile Data Market (OMDM).
Verify the production sourcing workflow and comparable retail evidence before
submitting price-led creative. Do not turn it into “cheapest”, guaranteed savings,
direct wholesale access for consumers, or a live customer bidding feature.

## Store-description paragraph

Openline works with multiple leading network partners, not just one. If your service
needs a different route, we can try to provide a replacement eSIM profile where an
alternative is available. Behind our plans, the Openline Mobile Data Market (OMDM)
compares supplier offers to help us source data at better prices and build better-value
travel plans. Network options depend on your destination and plan, and a profile
change may require setup on your phone.
