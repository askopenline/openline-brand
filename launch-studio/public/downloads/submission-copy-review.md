# Openline submission copy review

Reviewed 8 October 2026. Scope: English screenshot overlays and store listing
copy in the separate Apple/Google submission drafts, not certification of the app.

## Editorial changes

The copy is shorter and more natural while staying tied to the visible feature.
“Find your next destination” became “Where to next? Find your eSIM.” because
Openline helps select connectivity, rather than functioning as a destination guide.
“Activation information close” became “purchase code and activation details together.”
Usage wording avoids real-time, accuracy or signal-quality promises.

Apple's final frame now reads “Our market. Your choice.” The supporting sentence
still names the Openline Mobile Data Market (OMDM) and describes supplier comparison,
without making a “more choice,” cheapest or lower-price comparison.
Google's last frame remains a simple plan-choice benefit, with the factual OMDM
explanation in the full description.

The approved price-led marketing master is unchanged and is not the submission copy.
Both submission descriptions use short, readable sections and retain device,
fair-use, separate-purchase and home-carrier-charge information.

## Policy assessment

- Apple requires metadata to accurately reflect the actual experience. Unavailable
  services, false prices and misleading claims remain grounds for action. This
  revision avoids promotional price comparisons but still needs factual product
  verification. [Apple App Review Guidelines, §2.3](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- Screenshot overlays are permitted, but the screenshots must show the app in use.
  Our design references still need replacement with matching release-build captures.
  [Apple App Review Guidelines, §2.3.3](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- Apple descriptions allow 4,000 characters, promotional text 170 characters and
  keywords 100 bytes. Packaging validates these limits and the configured name/
  subtitle limits; passing a length check does not prove a claim.
  [Apple platform version information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information/)
- Google screenshot guidance recommends avoiding price-promotional overlays and
  limiting tagline area. The Google variant retains those conservative choices.
  These recommendations must not be misrepresented as automatic rejection rules.
  [Google preview-asset guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en)

## Still blocks a full compliance statement

- Native captures and release-feature verification, including purchase-code access,
  setup behavior, catalog contents and usage reporting.
- Evidence that OMDM performs the supplier comparison described at launch.
- Source UI still includes sample prices, “BEST VALUE” and “Unlimited data / full
  speed / hotspot included.” These were not silently removed from reference images.
  Correct any unsupported wording in the real app before recapture.
- Verified fair-use terms, supported devices, privacy/support URLs, account/deletion
  flows and build-specific reviewer access.
- Final store-console validation and review. No app or listing has been submitted.

Verdict: polished, lower-risk submission wording; not certified store compliance.
