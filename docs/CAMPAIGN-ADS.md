# Openline Campaign Ads

Twenty cinematic travel stories, with narrated everyday uses for mobile data and Openline travel eSIM planning.

[Open the Final batch](https://brand.openline.com/#/ads).

## Editions

- **Final batch:** 20 voiced ads, 22.08 seconds each.
- **Voiced campaign:** 10 earlier voiced ads.
- **Earlier work:** 30 earlier short ads.
- **All ads:** 60 collections. Original variants remain available in Archives.
