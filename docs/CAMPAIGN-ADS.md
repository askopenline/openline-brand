# Openline Campaign Ads

This document details the campaign advertisement assets available within the Openline brand library, accessible at [brand.openline.com/#/ads](https://brand.openline.com/#/ads).

## Active Short Ads

There are 40 active short ads in the current collection. This includes 30 earlier ads and 10 new voiced travel stories. The travel stories are approximately 22.1 seconds in duration.

## Voiced Travel Stories

The 10 new voiced travel stories feature:

*   Revised word-pop captions.
*   Spaced logo, QR code, and openline.com outros.
*   An AI circle disclosure.

Previous editions of these ads are retained in the Archives section of the brand library.

## Content Representation

It is important to note that the generated scenes within these ads are not actual customer footage, application footage, or performance footage. They are illustrative and do not represent real-world scenarios.

## Brand Colors in Campaigns

Campaign materials utilize a specific brand color token:

*   **Campaign Orange**: `#FF6418`

This is distinct from the native orange (`#FF6518`) and artwork orange (`#FF6616`) tokens, ensuring precise color application across different brand elements.
