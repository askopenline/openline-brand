# Openline logo library

The [Logos section](https://brand.openline.com/#/logos) collects the artwork already held throughout the brand library and its archives. It does not replace the approved app icon or silently promote review concepts to approved artwork.

## Browse by form

- **Icons only:** standalone marks and app-icon resources.
- **Icons + text:** horizontal and stacked lockups, plus the HTML website/blog combinations.
- **Text only:** the original path-based wordmark and its exported colourways.
- **Animated:** original loading motion and existing video/GIF treatments, including sound editions.
- **Website / HTML:** isolated, script-free examples of the observed live website and blog header logos.
- **Archive variants:** historical artwork and previously indexed formats.

Use Collections for visual families, or All files for individual sizes and formats. Search, format and review-status filters remain available; existing downloads are not renamed or removed.

## Website and blog typography

The headers observed on [openline.com](https://openline.com) and [blog.openline.com](https://blog.openline.com) on October 28, 2026 use the original raster icon beside live Play Bold text. The blog adds a divider and “the blog.” These observed headers are static, not animated.

[Implementation notes and downloadable source](../logo/web-headers/README.md) document the original sizes, fonts, portable-preview differences and source URLs. The original vector wordmark remains a separate artwork family.

## Preservation

The complete public inventory remains in [PUBLIC-ASSETS.json](../brand-library/PUBLIC-ASSETS.json), with hashes and URLs. Large existing video assets remain on the brand site and are retrievable using the hydration tool; source, documentation, website-header assets and the latest small tutorial MP4 are committed to GitHub.
