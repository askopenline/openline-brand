## Limits and provenance

All presenters are fictional and AI-generated. Some synthetic cadence remains; this is not a claim of human-indistinguishable footage. Automated audio review and decoded-frame checks are not a human listening audition.

Veo rejected new Hellen01 and Hellen03 generations without charge. Those references were not retried through another provider, and their earlier clips were not relabeled as upgrades. Gary02 uses one targeted retake; both paid takes remain accounted for.

Native generated audio can include quiet ambience or music. Downloadable presenter WAVs contain the complete leveled audio, not isolated dry voice stems. Earlier Hellen03 retains its prior below-neck clean-plate cleanup; the new Veo compositions do not use it.

The full 60–90-second tutorial still requires presenter selection, real iPhone recordings and product-specific activation validation. The approved 43.5% icon remains unchanged.
