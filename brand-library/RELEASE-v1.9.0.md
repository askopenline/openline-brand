# Openline Brand Studio v1.9.0

Three orange treatments replace the five-direction selection:
Original orange (retained), Airy orange (centred, upright), and Bold orange
(larger typography and interface crops).

The non-orange directions are removed from the current preview and download
packages. Six screenshots per platform remain, giving 36 current RGB PNGs.

The stronger story stays deliberately compact: screenshot 2 says
“Big networks. More choice.” and one paragraph in each description communicates
network quality, choice and fit for the traveller's budget. The other five
screens keep the approachable mass-market travel story.

No unverified “cheapest”, quantified savings, switching or universal multi-network
access claim is added. Launch partner evidence and native captures remain required.
The final 43.5% app icon and actual competitor references are unchanged.

The official wordmark replaces the small text-built header lockup. It is larger
and white on every orange background, with the black version on cream frames.
Its proportions are preserved; no AI-generated or retyped logo is used.
