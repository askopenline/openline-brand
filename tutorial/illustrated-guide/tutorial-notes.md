# Install your Openline eSIM

This complete illustrated example pairs Hellen's static office portrait with a single continuous
synthetic narration and animated phone walkthroughs. It is not a live recording or a lip-synced
presenter video. Phone interfaces are illustrations and vary by device, OS and plan.

## Follow along
- Choose QR **or** manual entry, not both.
- Use your own supplied installation details, never the example values in the video.
- Follow your purchased plan's activation, validity and roaming instructions.
- Home-provider calls and texts may still incur charges.
- Test internet access by briefly disabling Wi-Fi and opening a webpage.
- Contact Openline support before deleting an eSIM.
- Android follows similar principles; Samsung and Pixel menu names differ.
