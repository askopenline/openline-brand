Decision made to transition from AI-generated tutorials to human-produced content. The final tutorial will be created by a professional. No specific individual or completion date is set yet. All previous AI examples, including the latest 150-second illustrated guide, static Hellen portrait, faster synthetic voice, and animated mockup screens (not real iPhone recordings), are to be archived as development references.

AI tutorial production is stopped. The existing concepts are retained as references, not as the finished public installation tutorial. No professional is represented as already hired, and no delivery date is promised.

## Creative direction

Use human performance and handcrafted production for the final tutorial. The latest reference establishes the preferred pacing and composition, not a requirement to reproduce the AI presenter.

- Full-height presenter on the left, without a header or margins.
- Large, coherent animated phone views on the right, with concise readable instructions.
- QR and manual installation as alternatives, checked recaps, data settings, roaming and Android guidance.
- Natural, consistent narration; refined highlighted captions rather than full-width caption bands.
- Original Openline icon motion with sound, large intro/outro with fades.
- Closing link and scannable QR: https://openline.com/install. The owner handles that route; verify it before release.

## Content and production checks

Confirm current instructions, plan activation rules, device-specific labels and roaming conditions with the Openline product owner. Illustrations must not be represented as actual app/device recordings. Use authorized demo data for real screen capture, redact identifiers and codes, and secure performer and media rights.

The current examples are AI-generated development references, not claims about real customer experiences. Raw provider requests, receipts, credentials and internal task identifiers are intentionally excluded from this public handoff.
